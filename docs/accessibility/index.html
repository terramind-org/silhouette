Accessibility

We want this site to work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard or other assistive technology. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we do

  • Pages declare their language, use real headings and links, and can be used with a keyboard alone.
  • Every picture and recording has a text description. The recordings have no sound, so there is nothing to caption.
  • You can pause any recording in the docs by clicking it, or by tabbing to it and pressing Enter or Space.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the home page shows its content without the loading animation, and the recordings in the docs stay paused with their controls shown.

What does not work yet

  • The home page plays its loading animation again every few seconds and has no pause button. Turning on reduced motion on your device stops it.

Tell us about a problem

If something here does not work for you, email legal@terramind.com. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we will reply.
